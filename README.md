# FirstNationsCourt.ca

A plain-language public website explaining First Nations Court (BC's Indigenous sentencing courts) for Indigenous people, future clients of the Indigenous Community Legal Clinic, and law students starting the ICLC externship.

Plain HTML and CSS, no build step. Open index.html in a browser to view locally.

Content rules live in CLAUDE.md and are not optional: every factual claim traces to FACTS.md (each entry carries its source URL) or to AUTHOR-NOTES.md, and the style rules (plain language, no em dashes, no bold in copy, banned phrases) are enforced in QA. If you change content, follow CLAUDE.md.

Deployment steps are in DEPLOY.md. The launch checklist for the author is in LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md.
