Why this site exists

About this site

The information about First Nations Court is scattered across many websites, and most of it is written in dense legal language. This site pulls it together and says it plainly.

FirstNationsCourt.ca was made for Indigenous people who are facing a charge and want to know if this court might be for them, and for clients of the Indigenous Community Legal Clinic. Law students starting at the clinic may find it useful too, and a separate section for them may come later.

This site was created by Ash Akbari, a UBC law student who worked with clients through the Indigenous Community Legal Clinic and attended First Nations Court in New Westminster.

How this site was checked

Every fact on this site was traced to the official sources listed below, and the checking was done in August 2026. Anything that could not be verified was left out.

The firsthand details about the New Westminster court, like what the room at the Inn at the Quay feels like, come from attending that court in person.

The fine print

This site is general legal information, not legal advice. Every case is different, so talk to a lawyer about your own situation.

The site speaks for no Nation and no court. Ceremony and protocol differ between communities and between court locations, and the only people who can speak to them are the communities themselves.

Phone numbers, addresses, and schedules change. Call before you travel.
